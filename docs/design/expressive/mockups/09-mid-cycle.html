A plan that starts mid-cycle

Today the app counts whatever the phone recorded since the cycle start and marks the plan “Estimated” if something is missing. You cannot say “start later” or type a number. Three ways to choose, shown in the flow, the editor and on the Plans screen. Example: cycle starts on the 1st, today is 15 Oct.

You always see which kind of number it is. A small note on the plan says “7.5 GB entered on 15 Oct”. A plan that starts later is a muted, dashed tile with a date badge: “Starts 1 Nov”. It never goes amber or coral and never raises an alert.

The question only appears when today is not the first day of the cycle. After the entered number, the plan keeps following you to the end of the cycle; the next cycle starts from zero by itself.
