Plans
Front card + tiles. The front plan keeps the full story (ring, what is left, days, forecast). Every other plan is a compact tile: small wavy ring, name, one number, one line. Colour still tells the state (lilac fine, amber nearly out, coral over the cap, mint calm, plain dark for a plan with plenty left), and warning tiles keep their burst "!" icon. One tile can be wide when it earns it. Archived plans collapse into a dashed tile instead of a toggle. The button is a floating pill: New plan and +.
Questions this raises for the design

1. Which plan is the front card?
My suggestion: the plan that needs your attention most (nearest to running out), not just the first one you created. A pin would let you override it.

2. What does a tap do?
A tile opens the plan editor (as today). The editor is the next screen, and I would like it to open as a full-screen sheet with the same colour as the tile you tapped.

3. How many fit?
The grid scrolls. The front card plus six tiles fit in about two screens.

The small round "5" top right is a count of plans, like the avatar slot in Wallet; it is optional.
