Floating navbar: compact, centred, never shifting

The bar now hugs its three items instead of spanning the screen. Every version keeps the same total width whichever tab is selected, so the bar never moves or resizes; only what is inside it changes. Each is shown on Home, on Apps, and on Plans, where a round + (new plan) sits beside it.

Touch targets stay at least 48 dp tall. Icon-only is the smallest but loses the labels; Google's research found removing labels hurt usability, so I would not pick it unless the icons feel obvious to you. Names are still read aloud by TalkBack in all three.
