Palette checkpoint: 12 themes, light and dark

Each theme is the Home you approved, in its dark and light versions, with the real contrast numbers after our rules are applied (text at least 4.5:1, marks and rings at least 3:1). Yellow lines list every colour we had to change from the community palette to pass, so you can see how far each theme moved. Dusk, Ember and Monochrome are ours; the rest follow the community palettes (MIT, to be verified). Monochrome keeps to greys: warning and over-cap states are shown by tone plus the icon, never colour. Dracula and Nord light are our own derivations; Dracula's official light theme is in the paid Dracula PRO, so we do not copy it.
