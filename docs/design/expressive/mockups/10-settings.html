Data actions become a 2x2 tile grid (the same bento language as Plans): Export CSV, Export JSON, Create backup, Restore backup, each in its own colour, so a risky action like Restore is coral. Background gets a mint explanation card with one clear button. About shows the brand mark, the version, the privacy sentence and two connected buttons.

Tap Appearance to open the picker you saw earlier (mode, 12 themes, wallpaper colours, AMOLED). The Haptics switch (a light tick when the navbar snaps) moves into that Appearance screen, under AMOLED.
