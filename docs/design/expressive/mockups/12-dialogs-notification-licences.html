The floating navbar, dialogs, notification and licences

The last set of screens. Dusk theme. Each follows the same shapes and type as the screens you already approved.

1 · The floating navbar

Home
Resting. The selected item is a lilac pill with its label; the others are icons. Solid surface (the content no longer shows through, which fixes the see-through complaint from QA).
Home
Dragging. The pill stretches like elastic between two items while you drag, then snaps to the nearest one with a light tick (haptics). With animations off it jumps with no stretch.
Plans
Plans selected. Three tabs only. It slides away when you scroll down and returns when you scroll up, as today.

2 · Dialogs: icon, tall title, big pill buttons

All dialogs get a shaped icon, a tall title and pill buttons; the dangerous action is coral; choices are connected groups. They stay usable with the keyboard open at large font sizes (the fix from M6 is kept).

3 · The live notification (Android 16 live update)

The system draws notifications, so the look is partly Android's. We control the title, text, the progress bar with its colour, the status-bar chip and the icon. The chip only shows on Android 16.1 and newer; older phones get an ordinary ongoing notification.

4 · Open-source licences

Instead of one long block of text, a short list grouped by what each thing is, each with its licence as a coloured pill. Tapping an entry opens its full licence text, always left-to-right (this also fixes the full stops that landed on the wrong side in right-to-left). Our own licence leads, in a lilac card.
