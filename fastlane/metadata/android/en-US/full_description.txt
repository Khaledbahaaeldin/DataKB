Emberbyte is an open-source Android data monitor designed to give you clear control over your mobile and Wi-Fi data usage without compromising your privacy.

Features:
• Real-Time Speed & Live Sampler: Monitor your download speed and remaining plan data directly from your notification drawer.
• Daily & Historical Totals: View total data consumption broken down by cellular and Wi-Fi networks.
• Per-App Breakdown: See exactly how much data each application consumes across daily and 2-hour measurement windows.
• Flexible Data Plans: Set custom billing cycles, per-SIM plan binding, add-on packs, free data windows (such as off-peak night data), and usage rollover.
• Personalized Forecast: Predict when your data quota will run out using an hourly consumption curve learned over the last 28 days.
• Usage Threshold Alerts: Receive timely warnings when reaching 50%, 80%, 90%, and 100% of your plan allowance.
• Per-App Budgets: Configure custom mobile data budgets for specific apps with warning notifications.
• Spike Detection: Identify unusual mobile data traffic spikes compared against a 14-day median baseline.
• "What If" Scenario Planner: Simulate hypothetical usage changes to preview projected run-out dates.
• Encrypted Local Backups: Export and restore your plans, budgets, and hourly usage history with password-based AES-256-GCM encryption.
• Home Screen Widgets: Keep track of remaining data and billing cycle status directly from your home screen.

Privacy Guarantee:
Nothing leaves your device. Emberbyte operates entirely offline: no telemetry, no tracking, and no external servers. Your usage statistics and configuration stay strictly on your phone.

Required Permissions & Why We Need Them:
• Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Required to query historical per-application data transfer buckets provided by Android's NetworkStatsManager.
• Installed Apps (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Required on Android 11+ to display app names and icons alongside data usage records.
• Phone State (READ_PHONE_STATE): Required on multi-SIM devices to read SIM subscription IDs and bind cellular traffic to the correct data plan.
• Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required to deliver threshold warnings, budget alerts, spike alerts, and live transfer sampler updates.

Known Limitations:
• Dual-SIM Traffic: Per-SIM historical traffic is estimated when multiple SIM cards are active.
• VPN Over Wi-Fi: Data routed through a VPN over Wi-Fi is counted twice by Android's network subsystem (once for the tunnel and once for the underlying interface).
• Data Lag: New usage traffic can take up to 5 minutes to appear in system usage buckets.
• Mobile-Only Analysis: Budgets and spike detection evaluate mobile cellular data only.
